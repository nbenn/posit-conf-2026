# Rehearsal plan

Seven days from Tuesday 8 to the talk on Tuesday 15 at 13:05. Roughly 30 to
40 minutes a day, split into two or three short sessions rather than one long
one. Sleep between sessions does part of the work.

## The ladder

Each slide is drilled up a ladder of six views. A rung is passed when you
get through it twice in a row without a miss. On a miss, drop one rung, not
to the bottom.

- **Full.** Read aloud, with the deck open, twice.
- **1 in 5.** Say it, tapping a blank only when stuck.
- **1 in 3.** Same.
- **1 in 2.** Same. More than one miss on a slide means back to 1 in 3.
- **First letters.** Recite from the letters, then cover them.
- **Openers.** First word of each sentence. Recite the slide from those alone.

Then from nothing, with the slide image in mind and the clicker in hand.
The unit is always the slide. Once a slide is up the ladder, drill the join
into the next slide: last sentence, click, first sentence.

## Day by day

### Tue 8 · freeze and record

- Print the sheets (`cards/drill.html`, print all) and the speaker cards.
- Two full read-throughs aloud with the deck, timed per slide. Fix any
  sentence you stumble on twice: rewrite it to be sayable. After tonight
  the wording is frozen, since every change costs the memory work done so far.
- Record one clean read at the target pace. This is the reference for
  the week.
- Evening: slides 7 (close) and 6 (specialty) up the ladder to 1 in 3.
  Backwards, so every later run moves into material you know best.

### Wed 9 · the back half

- Morning: retest 7 and 6 from first letters. Drop rungs as needed.
- Learn 5 (real app) and 4 (code) to 1 in 3. Drill 4 with the deck, since
  the five reveals are part of the wording: click, then speak.
- Evening: chain 4 through 7 once from the 1 in 3 sheet.
- Listen to the recording on any walk or commute.

### Thu 10 · the front half

- Morning: retest 4 to 7 from first letters.
- Learn 3 (block), 2 (what carries over) and 1 (play-doh). Slide 2 is the
  long one at 176 words; give it two separate sessions.
- Evening: first full run, from the 1 in 2 sheet, standing, with the
  clicker, timed. Write down the time and the misses per slide.

### Fri 11 · first letters only

- Three full runs spread over the day, from the first-letters sheet.
- After each run, drill only the slides that missed, from 1 in 3.
- Shadow the recording once: speak along with it, then half a beat ahead.

### Sat 12 · from nothing

- Three runs from nothing, standing, clicker, timed. Vary the room.
- Rehearse blanking: twice per run, stop mid-slide on purpose, breathe,
  and jump to the next opener. The recovery is a skill, drill it.
- Check the timer at the click into the real app slide. Target 2:40.
  Practise the short version of that slide once, so the cut is rehearsed
  and not improvised.

### Sun 13 · variance

- Two or three runs from nothing. One too fast, one whispered, one at
  pace. Runs at pace should land within ten seconds of each other.
- Write out the openers list from memory. Compare against the sheet.
- Hold the speaker cards for one run so the fallback is familiar.

### Mon 14 · taper

- Two runs at most, one in the morning and one late afternoon. Stop
  by the evening. More runs now add nerves, not accuracy.
- Confirm the venue setup: own laptop or shared machine, presenter view
  or not, clicker, timer. Fold the openers card into your pocket.

### Tue 15 · talk day

- One run in the morning, from nothing, at pace. Then no more.
- Before going up: the openers card once, silently. First sentence in
  your head. Click.

## What to protect

Only a few sentences have to come out as written, because they echo the
slide text or carry the argument. Everything else may flex, and if time is
short it may be dropped.

- The transition cue into each slide (the last sentence before a click).
- The takeaway echo on each slide, since the audience reads it while you say it.
- The contract line on the block slide, and the brick, stud, model mapping.
- The sentence that sets up the custom block on the real app slide.
- Both closing lines.

Cuttable if the timer says so, in this order: the layout sentence on the
real app slide, the rollout sentence on the same slide, the thanks line at
the close.
